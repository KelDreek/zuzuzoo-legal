Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

toEPUB is a local macOS application for converting supported book, comic, archive, image, and document files into EPUB files.

No data collection

toEPUB does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share personal information, usage data, diagnostics, or the contents of your files.

Your files stay on your Mac

Files are accessed only after you select or drag them into the app. Conversion takes place locally on your Mac. Your files are never uploaded to a server.

No tracking or third-party services

toEPUB does not use accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, cloud services, or third-party data collection services.

Local app preferences

The app stores your selected export-folder preference locally on your device. This preference is not transmitted to us or shared with anyone.

Your privacy choices

Because toEPUB does not collect personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, delete, or provide.
