Privacy Policy for Comic2EPUB Last updated: 04.09.2026 Comic2EPUB is a local macOS application for converting supported book, comic, archive, image, and document files into EPUB files. Comic2EPUB does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share personal information, usage data, diagnostics, or the contents of your files. Files are accessed only after you select or drag them into the app. Conversion takes place locally on your Mac. Your files are not uploaded to a server. Comic2EPUB does not use accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, cloud services, or third-party data collection services. The app stores your selected export-folder preference locally on your device. This preference is not transmitted to us or shared with anyone. Because Comic2EPUB does not collect personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, delete, or provide. If you have questions about this policy, contact: kazachonaksiarhei@gmail.com
